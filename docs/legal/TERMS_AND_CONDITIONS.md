## 6. Solar System Types & Equipment Options

The Platform facilitates evaluation of three primary categories of solar power installations:
1. **On-Grid (Grid-Tied) Systems:** Systems interconnected directly with the local electrical distribution utility (DISCOM) via a bi-directional net meter. On-grid systems do not supply power during grid power failures (anti-islanding protection).
2. **Off-Grid Systems:** Systems operating independently of the utility grid, incorporating battery energy storage systems (BESS) and charge controllers. Generation and backup duration depend strictly on battery capacity and state of charge.
3. **Hybrid Systems:** Systems connected to both the utility grid and battery energy storage, providing automatic failover during grid interruptions subject to battery inverter capacity.

You are responsible for understanding the operational limitations, maintenance requirements, and recurring battery replacement costs associated with off-grid and hybrid systems.

## 7. DCR vs. Non-DCR Panels & Government Subsidy Schemes

### 7.1 Domestic Content Requirement (DCR) Panels
- To qualify for Central Financial Assistance (CFA) / Capital Subsidy under the **PM Surya Ghar: Muft Bijli Yojana** (administered by the Ministry of New and Renewable Energy — MNRE), the solar photovoltaic modules used in residential installations must comply with strict **Domestic Content Requirement (DCR)** regulations (utilizing solar cells and modules manufactured in India by manufacturers listed on the Approved List of Models and Manufacturers — ALMM).
- **Non-DCR panels** (which may feature imported cells or non-ALMM listed components) may be commercially available at differing price points but are **strictly ineligible** for Central Government capital subsidies under national rooftop solar schemes.

### 7.2 Subsidy Estimates Are Non-Binding
- Any subsidy figures displayed in the App (such as benchmark subsidy brackets up to ₹30,000 for 1 kW, ₹60,000 for 2 kW, or ₹78,000 for 3 kW and above for residential systems) reflect publicly available guidelines from the National Portal for Rooftop Solar at the time of calculation.
- **Subsidy approval, disbursement timelines, and final sanctioned amounts are solely under the jurisdiction and discretion of the Ministry of New and Renewable Energy (MNRE), the National Portal, and the designated distribution licensee (e.g., TANGEDCO).**
- The Company does not disburse subsidies, does not guarantee subsidy approval, and shall bear no liability if an application is rejected, reduced, or delayed by government authorities.

## 8. Solar Lens Feature & AI Computer Vision Limitations

### 8.1 Functionality of Solar Lens
Solar Lens is an experimental computational assessment tool. It allows users to capture or upload up to four (4) photographs or video sweeps of residential rooms. These images are transmitted to cloud-based artificial intelligence vision models (including Google Gemini APIs) to identify visible electrical appliances, categorize loads, and compile an estimated connected electrical inventory.

### 8.2 Inherent Limitations of Computer Vision
You acknowledge and accept the following technical limitations:
- **Visual Evidence Only:** The AI analyzes only visible surfaces. It cannot detect hidden wiring, concealed appliances, sub-distribution boards, or equipment situated outside the camera's field of view.
- **Wattage & Rating Approximations:** Unless an appliance nameplate with clearly legible electrical ratings is distinctly captured, appliance wattages are mapped from typical default averages. These default figures may differ significantly from your appliance's actual peak or running wattage.
- **User Verification Mandatory:** The Platform requires you to review, correct, edit, add, or delete appliances identified by SolarLens. **You bear full responsibility for confirming the accuracy of your appliance inventory before relying on it for system sizing.**
- **No Electrical Audit Certification:** Solar Lens does NOT constitute a certified energy audit, certified load survey, or electrical safety inspection under the Central Electricity Authority (CEA) Regulations or the Indian Electricity Rules, 1956.
