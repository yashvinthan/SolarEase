# Google Play Store Screenshot Suite & Creative Specifications

**Target Resolution:** 1080 × 2400 pixels (9:20 aspect ratio, 300 DPI)  
**Palette:** Surface Warm Mineral `#F8F8F2`, Header `#EEF2E9`, Hairline `#D2DBD1`, Brand Primary Botanical Green `#245B3A`, Tariff Alert Warm Amber `#B45309`, On-Surface Slate `#17201B`.  
**Typography:** Roboto / Clean Android-Native Sans (Medium 500 for headlines, Regular 400 for captions, Bold 700 for currency figures).
