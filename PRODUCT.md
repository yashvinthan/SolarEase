# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

android

## Users

Indian households managing electricity bills and considering, planning, or tracking a residential solar installation.

## Product Purpose

SolarEase brings bill tracking, home energy readings, solar assessment, cost estimation, payment choices, installation tracking, and ongoing solar performance into one mobile experience.

## Positioning

It connects the practical household electricity journey—from meter reading and TNEB bill status through an actionable solar proposal and installed-system monitoring—in a single Android app.

## Operating Context

People use the app on Android phones, often while reviewing a bill, reading a meter, comparing an estimate, or coordinating an installation. The assessment and vendor flow is a twelve-step decision journey.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- TNEB bill-status and bill-history workflows.
- Meter scanning, manual readings, and consumption visualisations.
- Solar Lens appliance audit, assessment, estimate, payment, order, and installation tracking.
- Profile, analytics, recommendations, dashboard, financial, environmental, and system-detail routes.
- Legal, compliance, and payment information must remain clear and available.
- Product facts are inferred from the existing implementation; no claims, costs, or energy outputs may be fabricated by the interface.

## Brand Commitments

SolarEase remains the product name. The requested direction is elegant, calm, modern, premium, and Android-native.

## Evidence on Hand

- Existing Compose routes and domain models under `app/src/main/java/com/example`.
- Existing solar/vendor illustration assets under `app/src/main/res/drawable*`.
- No user-approved photographic brand assets or external performance claims are on hand.

## Product Principles

- Put the household's next decision ahead of supporting detail.
- Make energy, cost, and progress legible at a glance without implying unverified data.
- Keep high-stakes bill, payment, privacy, and legal information explicit.
- Use one coherent, accessible Android-native visual language across the full journey.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Support increased Android font scale, touch targets of at least 48 dp, meaningful screen-reader labels, dark theme, and readable state contrast.
