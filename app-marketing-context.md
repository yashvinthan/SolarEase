## 2. Value Proposition & Personas
- **Problem:** Indian urban and semi-urban households struggle with escalating bi-monthly TNEB (Tamil Nadu Electricity Board) electricity bills, confusing slab tariffs, complex government rooftop solar subsidies (PM Surya Ghar Muft Bijli Yojana), and fragmented, unverified local solar contractors.
- **Target Audience:**
  1. *Suburban Homeowners & Independent House Owners* (30–55 yrs) in Tamil Nadu and South India facing high tier-slab electricity bills (>500 units/cycle).
  2. *Green Energy Adopters & Technologists* seeking live generation monitoring and carbon offset tracking.
  3. *Small Commercial & Residential RWA (Residents Welfare Association) Decision Makers* evaluating CAPEX vs. OPEX solar payback.
- **Unique Differentiator:** An all-in-one offline-first utility assistant that combines:
  1. Camera OCR meter scanning and automated bi-monthly TNEB tariff breakdown.
  2. "Solar Lens" on-device appliance audit (calculates individual appliance wattage and rooftop load).
  3. 12-step guided solar feasibility assessment with PM Surya Ghar subsidy deductions, payback period calculation, and localized verified vendor directory.
- **Elevator Pitch:** "SolarEase gives Indian homeowners total control over their electricity bills—from scanning their meter to claiming government subsidies and getting rooftop solar installed with verified local vendors."
